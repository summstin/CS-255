The client for the major project this course covered was a company called DriverPass, a company that wanted to provide a solution to increase the success rate of student drivers. To accomplish this, they wanted to have a web application developed that would provide resources to help prepare for an official driving exam. These resources include reference material as well as allowing students to make reservations with driving instructors employed by DriverPass. These resources become available to students upon purchasing packages available on the site.

I feel like I made really good improvements on my BRD for the second submission. I could understand the points made during feedback and tried to incorporate details in it after the fact to address all of them reasonably well. It is sometimes easy to overlook the necessity for certain details which is why it's always good to have a second opinion given on it. 

I feel like one part of the project I could have performed better was the work on the use case diagram. It could have been laid out in a more organized way. I also feel like the Gantt chart made for the project was lacking in its design quality. Perhaps I could have used a tool that felt a bit more intuitive to use. I also could have simply dedicated more time to getting the charts to look more appealing or went with another approach.

When reading through the interview, I found it helpful to take note of any specific requirements as I came across them and trying to imagine how they might be incorporated in the system by thinking about how similar features from other sites work. I tried to make sure I didn't make too many assumptions as perhaps the client would have had a different vision in mind. It is important to make sure that when these systems are being designed that it still remains within the vision of what they're asking for. If the end product strays too far from what they ask, they might end up not being as satisfied with the outcome. 

When designing software, I like to break things apart into different functions or parts. I also try to imagine what kind of entities or objects each of those parts would require. I then try to imagine how each of those parts would interact with each other and how user intent is translated into action from start to finish. In the future, for a similar project, I would utilize more communication with the client. Since I only had the interview transcript to go off, I could not reach out to seek clarification on exact specifics so a lot of the detail work was left to interpretation.
